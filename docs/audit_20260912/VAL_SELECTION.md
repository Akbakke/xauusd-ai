## 2. Natural censoring, gaps, compute limits, and patience

- The adapter distinguishes continuous M1, declared market closure, unknown source gaps, and split end (`unified_exit_random_access_val_rollout_v1.py:572–614`). It rejects a lifecycle crossing an unknown gap and rejects premature stopping. Neither the 512-state/history capacity nor an economic terminal is silently introduced.
- At a boundary, the model can still EXIT_NOW. If it instead HOLDs without an observed successor, the result is `RIGHT_CENSORED_SPLIT_END` or `RIGHT_CENSORED_UNKNOWN_SOURCE_GAP`; it is not a forced close or a zero-return completed trade (`unified_exit_random_access_val_evaluator_v1.py:1000–1037`).
- Native complete-VAL admission accepts only `EXITED` and natural `RIGHT_CENSORED_SPLIT_END`, requires all 11016 side outcomes, and rejects compute truncation/unknown gaps (`unified_exit_native_candidate_campaign_v1.py:97–130`). A 4200-second invocation limit is resumable: evaluator `887–910`; the CLI binds `resumable_wall_limit=True` at `run_unified_exit_random_access_val_v1.py:588–598`. A hard total-forward/state guard yields explicitly non-authoritative truncation, not terminal PnL (`val_evaluator:878–886,1053–1057`).
- Only actually selected sides determine whether policy cash Bps is authoritative; unselected counterfactual censoring alone does not veto it (`unified_exit_entry_policy_evaluation_v1.py:92–114`). This avoids penalizing a policy for a side it did not select.
- Known tradeoff: even one selected censored side makes the epoch metric unavailable (`trainer:12241–12247,12844–12864`), and failed admission/unavailable metric increments patience (`12989–13007`). Five such epochs can stop without a selected checkpoint; five after a prior admitted epoch retain that prior best. This behavior was confirmed by the parent as previously agreed. It is not a reason to modify or restart the active frozen run. Do not replace null Bps with the closed-only mean or force June-end closes to manufacture eligibility.

## 3. EMA and target snapshot consistency

- One immutable validation snapshot per completed TRAIN epoch is created from saved EMA shadow, with matching optimizer-step count, online-model hash, exact buffers, and frozen target hash (`trainer:2158–2204`). Existing snapshot collisions are checked against model/target hashes (`2214–2228`).
- Native evaluation temporarily installs that EMA and binds its actual model-state digest (`12192–12224`; `gx1/contracts/unified_exit_random_access_val_checkpoint_v1.py:114–153`). Entry representations, Entry action Q, and Exit rollout use this same bound model (`run_unified_exit_random_access_val_v1.py:570–617`). The target model is used only for frozen-target diagnostic labels; its hash must match the snapshot (`572–573`).
- EMA updates only parameters, copies buffers exactly, and restores the online state after evaluation (`trainer:5766–5776,5807–5842`). Selected top-k state comes from the same EMA shadow, with the corresponding frozen target (`12927–12943`). No raw/EMA selection mismatch was found.
- Selection maximizes the sole finite net-Bps monitor; ties do not reset patience because improvement is strictly greater than min-delta zero (`entry_candidate_checkpoint_policy_v1.py:89–99`). Top-k sorting is deterministic and admission is applied before candidate checkpoint retention (`trainer:12866–12948`).

## 4. Health is measured, but it is not proof of useful cooperation

The native path does not hardcode health to green. It records per-coordinate sum/squared sum/min/max and entropy from actual Entry outputs and actual active Exit states (`unified_exit_random_access_val_evaluator_v1.py:298–419,478–519`). Exit gates are shared across sides; each active shared state is counted once (`315–330`). Native stats check units/shapes/finiteness and feed those moments into the existing health owner (`trainer:5513–5571`).

The admission rules are:

- Actual active-head/target observations over June, with at least the candidate minimum supervised rows and per-column output/target liveness (`run_unified_exit_random_access_val_v1.py:438–461,489–500`; `trainer:10255–10509,10527–10529`). Q agreement/loss remains diagnostic; it is not the selection score.
- Every cooperation route must have positive mean mass and positive finite entropy (`trainer:5384–5419`). There is no minimum practical mass, balance target, or ablation-improvement condition in this check. Nearly unused routes with tiny positive weights can therefore pass. A pass establishes these narrow numerical conditions, not that each family adds useful information or that Entry action distribution is healthy.
- Every Entry feature coordinate must have standard deviation above `1e-6`, and no observed feature gate may hit 0 or 2 (`trainer:5420–5455`; `unified_exit_gate_evidence_v1.py:18`). Entry remains strict. Exit may provisionally allow only static, positive, non-saturated feature multipliers; saturation/nonfinite/routing failures still block (`trainer:5618–5655,10562–10600`). Publication then requires separate selected-checkpoint input-influence evidence.

Observed seed concern: its Entry family/TF cooperation has mean effective-route count `1.0005963286052522`, and coordinate 25 is top for all 5508 Entry rows. Entry feature gates reached 2 in 31343 of 3877632 cells (`0.8083%`), so the seed would fail the current strict Entry saturation gate. Its Exit feature gates were unsaturated. These observations make future admission uncertain; they do not prove that the five-year optimizer cannot recover. Seed V34 lacks the newer per-coordinate moment fields and native candidate active-head evidence, so its complete future admission result cannot be reconstructed from those historical diagnostics alone.

Minor mathematical limitation: route entropy uses `clamp(min=1e-12)` on the probabilities themselves before `p*log(p)` (`val_evaluator:403–405`). For an exactly one-hot 8-way row this reports about `1.934e-10`, rather than zero. With one-hot choices changing across rows so every route has positive mean, the positive-entropy check alone does not reject per-row deterministic routing. This is not evidence that the current seed's near-collapse would be rejected by a corrected entropy formula, nor a demonstrated training blocker. If exact-zero entropy matters in a later authorized change, preserve the zero coefficients and clamp only the logarithm input.

## 5. Small economics discrepancy: the fill-to-first-decision minute

Source inspection indicates the reported net cash starts financing at the first Exit decision, while the position was filled one minute earlier. Entry price is M1 open (`economic_step_provider:414–421`), first Exit decision is bar start plus 60 seconds (`587`), EXIT_NOW financing is zero (`610`), and HOLD financing covers only decision-to-successor time (`625–648`). The evaluator initializes cash to zero and accumulates only HOLD/EXIT steps (`val_evaluator:110–125,277–290,1000–1037`). No initial ENTRY accrual is added on this native path.

Under the authority's continuous open-notional financing formula (`unified_exit_prospective_cost_policy_v1.py:251`), this omits 60 seconds of LONG financing: `540 annual bps * 60 / 31557600 = 0.001026694045174538 bps` per selected LONG trade. SHORT financing is deliberately clipped to zero (`prospective_cost_policy:330`), so this does not change the completed all-SHORT seed diagnostic. The parent independently confirmed the initial minute is actual open exposure and the state-zero lifetime summary includes it. No exclusion of that minute was found in the financing method.

The actual main `PROSPECTIVE_COST_POLICY_V1/parameter_authority.json` has annual risk penalties zero for both sides, so omitted initial risk accrual is currently exactly zero. The main `CAPITAL_HURDLE_V1/train_economics_readiness.json` has continuous annual hurdle `0.09531017980432487 = ln(1.1)`. An initial 60-second hurdle factor would be `0.9999998187881756`; omitting it changes utility by `1.812118244e-7` relative, or about `0.00001812` Bps on a 100-Bps continuation value. This common positive multiplier alone preserves LONG/SHORT/FLAT argmax. Unlike the missing cash financing, this factor's absence is explicitly encoded in the legacy first-post-fill target convention: `entry_fitted_q_v1.py:79–90` fixes gamma to 1 and transition reward to zero, and `294–295` directly copies first-state side values. It is therefore a declared target-time convention, not an accidental failed multiplication in the native adapter.

Classification: small source-level cash accounting discrepancy relative to elapsed financing from actual fill, with no evidence it renders the training unusable. The smallest eventual correction is an explicit one-time initial accrual (with corresponding TRAIN Entry target semantics), without charging it on every continuation or altering saved results. Resolve any intended decision-origin convention before making such a later change; this approximately 0.001-Bps bias alone does not justify discarding or changing the live frozen run.
