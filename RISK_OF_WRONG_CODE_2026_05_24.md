# GX1 current risk register

Updated 2026-08-03. The filename is historical; this content is current.

## Highest risks

1. **Host capacity.** `.wslconfig` is 32 GB/4 GB, but the active VM still
   reports about 43 GiB/8 GiB. Heavy work must remain blocked until restart
   applies the lower cap.
2. **Dataset PASS mistaken for edge.** V8/V13 and MTF schema-v3 are valid
   offline inputs. They prove no direction, Exit, precision or profitability.
3. **Partial smoke reused after reboot.** The interrupted epoch-3 attempt has
   no terminal bundle/event and is invalid. Scratch state cannot be resumed.
4. **Class or head collapse.** Earlier smoke evidence was almost all FLAT,
   with path heads near chance and weak specialist/family cooperation. A
   longer run must not be called progress unless the immutable gates pass.
5. **Overfit from full-stack capacity.** The architecture is intentionally
   rich. Candidate selection must use TRAIN/VAL only and prove untouched TEST;
   adding more features or layers is not an accepted response.
6. **Old launch state confused with current offline state.** The launch
   checkpoint still records historical schema-v2/no-dataset authority because
   nothing is launch-admitted. The handover must separately verify current
   offline V8/V13 schema-v3 evidence before presenting it as runnable input.
7. **Separate Entry/Exit drift.** Entry M5 and Exit M1 must retain one 513-field
   owner, eight specialists, split boundaries and TRAIN normalization. M1
   resolution and additive path are the only intentional Exit differences.
8. **Presence mistaken for influence.** Gate activity and non-constant heads
   do not prove that a route affects the decision. Margin ablation and local
   sensitivity remain mandatory.
9. **Manual direction leaks.** A threshold, filter, confluence score, cached
   decision or synthetic `FLAT`/`HOLD` would create a forbidden second policy.
10. **Artifact drift.** Paths selected by `latest`, glob, mtime or version
    order can silently mix lineages. Every consumer must revalidate exact
    immutable identities.
11. **Acceptance weakened after seeing results.** Thresholds, TEST selection
    and target definitions cannot move to make a failed run pass.
12. **Documentation drift.** Historical chronology belongs only in
    `DECISION_LOG.md`. Operative documents must point to the V8/V13 smoke
    boundary and the pending WSL safety gate.

## Required controls

- one executable handover that validates current offline recipe/artifact
  bindings before reporting `READY`;
- one capped heavy job at a time: 10 GiB RAM, 512 MiB swap, CPU 0–1;
- no heavy start while WSL reports the old host limit;
- exact model argmax for both decisions, with no fallback;
- complete 513+142+5 and 5×111 identity/liveness proof;
- same-bundle Entry/Exit training and candidate immutability;
- TRAIN/VAL-only selection and untouched TEST;
- route ablation plus 1,723 numeric and five categorical sensitivity proofs;
- terminal immutable evidence for every heavy attempt;
- evidence-retention owner for GX1_DATA deletion;
- launch remains `BLOCK` until current model, OOS and replay contracts pass.
